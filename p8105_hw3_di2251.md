The `ny_noaa` dataset has 2595176 rows and 7 columns, with one row per
station per day. It covers 747 New York weather stations from 1981-01-01
to 2010-12-31. Key variables are the station ID (`id`), the date,
precipitation (`prcp`), snowfall (`snow`), snow depth (`snwd`), and
maximum and minimum temperature (`tmax`, `tmin`).

Missing data is a major issue. 43.7% of `tmax` values and 43.7% of
`tmin` values are missing, along with 5.6% of precipitation, 12.7% of
snowfall, and 22.8% of snow depth. Many stations appear to record only
precipitation and snow, not temperature.
